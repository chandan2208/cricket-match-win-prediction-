Methodology

CricPredic utilizes advanced machine learning architectures to synthesize match probabilities. Our engine analyzes over 50,000 historical T20 and ODI fixtures, incorporating real-time environmental data including:

  • Pitch surface friction and moisture levels
  • Atmospheric pressure and humidity interaction
  • Team archetype tactical advantages (Spin/Pace/Swing)
  • Player-specific head-to-head performance vectors

Privacy Policy

CricPredic is a privacy-first platform. We do not sell user data. All prediction history is stored securely to enhance your analytical experience. We use localStorage to preserve your visual theme preferences (Test vs. IPL) locally on your device.

Archives

The CricPredic Archive contains match data spanning the last decade of international cricket. Premium users can access detailed heatmaps and ball-by-ball analysis via the VIP tier.
